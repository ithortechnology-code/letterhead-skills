# Start here (no technical knowledge needed)

This folder holds your company's letters. You never need to open or edit the files inside by hand. Just ask the
assistant for what you want ("write a letter to the bank asking for ..., with signature and seal").

| Folder | What it is | Do you open it? |
|---|---|---|
| **generated/** | Every letter the assistant has made, drafts and finals. Open **index.html** in your browser to see them all, review one, and print or download the PDF. | Yes, to review |
| **approved/** | The final copies you have signed off. Open **index.html** here too. These are the ones to send. | Yes, to send |
| letters/ | The text of each letter (the assistant edits these) | No |
| assets/ | Your logo and signature files | Only to replace them |
| config.json | Company name, address, numbers, signatory | No, ask the assistant to change it |

**Everyday flow**
1. Ask the assistant for a letter. It opens the draft in your browser.
2. Read it. Ask for changes in plain words ("make it shorter", "add the invoice number").
3. When it's right, say **"approve it"**. A final copy goes into **approved/**. Send that one.

**Good to know**
- Print from the page with: paper size A4, scale 100%, headers and footers off. Or use the **Download PDF** button.
- The signature and seal are only added when you ask for them.
- This folder is private to your company. Keep it that way: the signature image is in here.
